Technology meets

I'm Richmond Tetteh Osabutey, an Accra-based IT professional and visual storyteller with a passion for using technology and creativity to shape meaningful experiences.

My background in Information Technology, software development and live media production informs the way I approach photography: with curiosity, technical precision and an eye for the details that make every image feel intentional.
